About

A Retreat Above the Everyday

Cloudwood Resort is a premium getaway in Wayanad, created for travellers seeking space, calm, and a deeper connection with nature. Surrounded by the region’s hills and greenery, it offers a peaceful base for couples, families, and small groups.

Wake up to misty mornings, unwind in nature, and let Wayanad set the pace for your escape.

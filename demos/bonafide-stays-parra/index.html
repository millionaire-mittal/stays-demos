About

A Curated Collection of Goan Villas

The Bonafide Stays is a hospitality brand offering a small, considered collection of luxury villas in Parra, North Goa. Each property is designed to offer privacy, comfort, and a sense of place—whether you are travelling as a couple, with family, or with a group of friends.

At The Bonafide Stays, every villa is more than accommodation—it is a private Goan escape designed around space, comfort, and the freedom to unwind.

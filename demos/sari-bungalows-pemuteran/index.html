The place

The Sari Bungalows is a small, relaxed guesthouse in Pemuteran, set in a lush tropical garden just off North Bali’s main road. Guests consistently praise the clean, comfortable rooms, friendly staff, refreshing pool and tasty included breakfast. Its quiet location is close to Pemuteran Beach, restaurants, snorkelling trips and the diving area around Menjangan Island.

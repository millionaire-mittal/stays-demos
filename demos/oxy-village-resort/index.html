The experience

Slow down. Breathe. Be together.

Oxy Village Resort by De Verveil is a calm, green corner away from the noise of the city. Wide lawns, shady trees and homes built from bamboo, laterite and warm wood make it feel less like a hotel and more like a village of your own.

Spacious, comfortable rooms, attentive and personal service, good food and open space to play: everything is here so you can spend real, unhurried time with the people you love.
